# Vision & principles

## Vision

A Christian faith-based mental wellness mobile app that blends:

- **Immediate calming support** (panic relief, emotion-first entry) — comparable emotional safety to apps like Rootd
- **Sacred, Scripture-anchored meditation and prayer** — comparable depth to apps like Abide

Positioning: **high-end wellness (Calm / Headspace caliber) + biblical depth.**  
Never kitschy church clip-art. Never sparse “generic meditation.” Never fear-based religion product.

## Brand character: Grace

- **Name:** Grace  
- **Role:** Warm companion through onboarding and empty states  
- **Visual:** Gentle, approachable mascot (brain holding open Bible + soft cross/plus). Represents mental renewal through faith.  
- **Rules:** Never depict Jesus as a cartoon. Never use Grace for guilt or shame. Expressions: listening, hopeful, gentle smile.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Emotional safety** — Every screen must feel calm. No shock, no doom, no guilt.
2. **Scripture reverence** — Verses are set with care (distinct type treatment). Never walls of all-caps verses. Never weaponize Scripture.
3. **Emotion-first** — The primary path is “How do you feel?” → matching content. Not a rigid Day 1–7 course lock-in.
4. **Personalization is gentle** — Use onboarding data to greet and route; never “diagnose” harshly.
5. **Value-first monetization** — Soft paywall **after first completed practice**, not before the user feels helped.
6. **No aggressive sales** — No fake urgency, fake social proof, countdown timers, or fear FOMO.
7. **Crisis honesty** — SOS and Wisdom are support tools, **not** licensed therapy. Crisis signals → urge real-world help / emergency services.
8. **Light + dark themes** — Full dual theme; default **dark** (Nest). Light is Cooper.
9. **One screen, one job** — Clear hierarchy; cards as atoms; avoid nested cards.
10. **Stack independence** — Product truth lives in contracts and UX, not in Expo/Swift/Flutter APIs.

## Tone of voice

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Warm, calm, clear | Preachy, scolding |
| Short paragraphs | Sermon outlines |
| “You’re not alone” | “You should feel guilty” |
| Invite rest | Hustle spirituality |
| Truth + grace | Shame + performance |
| One gentle next step | Command lists |

Wisdom AI framing: companion reflecting the *heart of Jesus*, not a historical Jesus voice clone. Prefer: “In the spirit of Christ…” / “Jesus’ way would invite you to…”

## Success metrics (product)

| Metric | Intent |
|--------|--------|
| First practice completion | User felt enough safety to finish one session |
| Day-7 retention | Habit of returning for emotion-based calm |
| Wisdom quality (subjective) | Replies feel pastoral, in-scope, not generic chatbot |
| Soft paywall conversion | After value, not forced at install |
| Support tickets re: tone | Near-zero complaints about pressure or fear tactics |

## Explicitly out of brand

- Fear/guilt imagery or copy  
- Shock visuals  
- Fake reviews or social proof  
- Aggressive urgency / fake discounts  
- Politics, prosperity hype  
- Clinical diagnosis language  
- Depicting Jesus as mascot
